# 2D素材生成说明

这些素材使用本任务可用的内置图像生成工具生成，并压缩为项目中的WebP文件。工具没有返回可核实的具体模型版本，因此不标注为“image2.5”。角色和服务亭输出带透明背景；地图不含烘焙文字和角色，课堂任务由页面叠加。

## 小镇地图（game-map.webp）

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background for a 2D browser educational adventure game. Wide 16:9 continuous playable town map, orthographic top-down with slight frontal visibility of buildings, hand-painted flat 2D storybook style, crisp dark teal ink outlines, warm ivory paths, sage grass, teal river, coral and mustard roofs, subtle matte gouache paper texture. Cheerful Chinese elementary-school town festival. Make a distinct welcoming entrance at bottom-center; a little activity notice kiosk with magnifying-glass ornament at mid-left; a botanical discovery greenhouse upper-left; an open-air performance plaza upper-center; and a visitor recommendation cottage at mid-right decorated with tiny science, sport and art motifs. Three visually distinct broad paths lead from entrance to plaza: west riverside path crossing a small footbridge, central path, east garden path. All paths unblocked and wide enough for overlay character sprites; buildings should be well spaced with visible doorways. Balanced detailed but uncluttered landscape, clear silhouettes and strong game readability at 1366x768. NO people, NO characters, NO text, NO numbers, NO logos, NO UI, NO signage, NO badges, NO arrows, NO roadblock. This is a map layer, not a poster or screenshot. Absolutely 2D, not 3D or isometric voxel.
```

## 向导背面行走姿态（guide-back.webp）

参考原有透明角色 `guide.webp` 生成：同一名短黑发、青绿色外套、珊瑚色领巾、芥末色斜挎包、深色短裤和绿棕色运动鞋的五年级向导，从背后看、全身、向地图上方迈步。保持原角色比例、手绘水粉与深青色描边，透明背景，无文字或其他人物。

## 向导侧面行走姿态（guide-side.webp）

参考原有透明角色 `guide.webp` 生成：同一名向导从侧面看、朝右迈步，全身，保留服装、包、面部年龄与手绘风格，透明背景。向左行走时由网页水平翻转这张侧面素材。

## 算法路径地图（game-map-paths.webp）

以内置图像生成工具编辑原小镇地图，保留建筑、入口、演出广场、公告处、温室、推荐屋及道路的位置；将西侧河边路、中心路、东侧花园路分别做成琥珀、青绿、淡紫铺装，并加分叉节点纹样。此图仍是美术底图，角色是否能通过由 `walk-map.cjs` 的道路格子决定。

## 行走补间姿态（guide-front-pass.webp、guide-back-pass.webp、guide-side-pass.webp）

以原向导角色的各方向姿态作参考，生成同服装、同画风、不同迈步位置的透明立绘。页面行走时交替切换同方向两帧；向左用侧面帧水平翻转。浏览器的减少动态效果设置下不强制播放帧动画。

## 叶片图卡（leaf-ginkgo.webp、leaf-maple.webp、leaf-pine.webp）

生成黄色扇形叶、红色掌状叶与绿色针叶三张无文字植物图卡。图片仅用于小学课堂观察与教学模拟，未作为训练数据或真实图像识别服务的输入。
